Grok Reels Library

Pure browser tool for browsing your vertical (9:16) Grok generations. No extension required.

Install the bookmarklet

Drag the button below to your bookmarks bar (Safari: Bookmarks → Show Bookmarks Bar if needed).

⭐ Reels Library
Safari tip: If the bookmarks bar is hidden, go to View → Show Bookmarks Bar (or ⌘+Shift+B).

How to use

  1. Log in to Grok and open grok.com/library or grok.com/imagine.
  2. Click the Reels Library bookmark you just added.
  3. A floating panel appears with a 9:16 grid of your generations. Search by prompt or filter Image/Video.

The panel uses your existing login session. No files are downloaded unless you click an item and choose to save it.
